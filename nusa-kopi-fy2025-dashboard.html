Section 5

Revenue by stream

Six trading streams before discounts, per Schedule 1. Share is stated against the sum of the six streams, which is the figure that reconciles to revenue after discounts.

FY2024 FY2025 Gross revenue by stream, RM

Note on mix. Every one of the six streams grew by 19.45%, to five significant figures, so the revenue mix is unchanged to two decimal places and the delivery channel's share of revenue is identical in both years at 21.356%. Uniform growth of that precision across six independent streams — including franchise royalties, which are a 5% royalty on third-party franchisee turnover — is worth confirming as genuine stream-level data rather than an allocation of the group growth rate. Note on the gross total. Schedule 1 states gross revenue before discounts of RM52,480,000, but the six streams sum to RM52,462,474. The RM17,526 difference is the group-level loyalty deferral and gift card settlement shown separately in the outlet reconciliation (RM17,525). The stream sum less discounts of RM1,235,882 gives revenue of RM51,226,592 exactly.

Section 7

Operating leverage

Revenue growth against operating expense growth. Where a cost block grows more slowly than revenue, leverage is gained; where it grows faster, leverage is lost.

Verdict

Leverage was gained.

Revenue grew 19.45% while total operating expenses grew 18.06% — a gap of 1.39 percentage points. Strip out depreciation and amortisation, which are a function of the estate build rather than of trading, and cash operating expenses grew 16.15%, a gap of 3.30 points. Operating profit consequently grew 42.67%, more than twice the rate of revenue.

Section 8

Detailed statement of profit or loss

Every general ledger account carrying a balance in the statement of profit or loss, in ledger order, with account codes from the Company's chart of accounts. This is the reference table.

Reading the columns. Amounts are stated on a signed basis — income positive, expenditure in brackets — so every column casts down to the subtotal beneath it. % of revenue is the unsigned ratio of the line to revenue in its own year. Change RM is the FY2025 amount less the FY2024 amount on that same signed basis, so a bracketed change on an expense line means the expense grew. Change % is the movement in the size of the line, so a positive figure on an expense line also means the expense grew. Both therefore point the same way. Casting differences. The subtotals shown are those stated in the source document, which is what ties to the primary statement. In eight instances the component lines cast to RM1 away from the stated subtotal: cost of sales, personnel, occupancy, administrative and finance costs and other income in FY2025, and personnel and selling and distribution in FY2024. Each is a rounding artefact of the source and none exceeds one Ringgit.

Section 9

Taxation

The effective rate of 21.28% sits 2.72 points below the 24% statutory rate, having been 4.57 points above the pre-tax result last year. Schedule 10 provides the full reconciliation.

Effective rate against statutory

The effective rate is the total tax charge over profit before taxation. FY2024's rate exceeded the statutory rate; FY2025's falls below it. The whole of that 4.57 point swing is explained by the reconciling items opposite, of which capital allowances is by far the largest.

Rate reconciliation — statutory 24% to effective 21.28%

Expressed as a percentage of profit before taxation. Capital allowances of RM4,118,600 were claimed against book depreciation of property, plant and equipment and amortisation of intangibles totalling RM2,515,400 — an excess of RM1,603,200, worth RM384,768 of tax at 24%. That excess is a timing benefit, not a permanent one: the deferred tax charge has already risen from RM23,671 to RM186,300 as it begins to reverse. Schedule 10 provides reconciling items for FY2025 only; the FY2024 column shows the total charge and effective rate, which are the only comparative figures the document gives.
